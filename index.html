Abstract

Foley sound synthesis is a one-to-many problem: each scene category admits many valid realizations, yet only a few hours of labeled audio are available, making it hard to preserve sample diversity without losing scene fidelity. We propose a mel-domain framework based on Schrödinger Bridge Conditional Flow Matching (SB-CFM). The Schrödinger bridge is the entropy-regularized form of dynamic optimal transport; its marginal path smooths the transport interpolation with a kernel that vanishes at both endpoints, regularizing the velocity regression while matching the data distribution exactly. Scene identity enters a two-dimensional velocity U-Net as a class embedding, and the frame-level RMS envelope of the target is injected for explicit temporal control. Generation integrates the resulting velocity field as an ordinary differential equation; no score network is trained and no stochastic sampling is used. On DCASE 2023 Task 7, SB-CFM attains a Fréchet Audio Distance of 2.75, a 42.3% reduction over the strongest baseline, with the lowest Kernel Audio Distance and temporal error and the highest density and coverage of any system compared. Ablations at fixed guidance decompose this margin along two axes, the coupling and the bridge, varying one at a time: the intra-class optimal-transport coupling supplies the larger part — 54% of the improvement from independent pairing to the full model — and the bridge the remaining 46%, up to an interior optimum at σ=0.2.

§ III — Proposed method

An entropy-regularized bridge in the mel domain, steered by an envelope

We generate in the log-mel domain. A standard-Gaussian source is coupled to a target spectrogram by an exact intra-class optimal-transport assignment, and the two are joined by the Schrödinger-bridge path — the entropy-regularized form of dynamic optimal transport, whose marginal is the OT displacement interpolation convolved with a Gaussian kernel that vanishes at both endpoints. The interior of the transport is smoothed, so the field the network must regress is better conditioned than the unregularized OT field, while source and data distributions are matched exactly. A single 2-D U-Net regresses the closed-form velocity; scene identity enters as a class embedding through zero-initialized adaptive group normalization, and the frame-level RMS envelope enters through block-wise FiLM, which controls when energy appears. We keep the formulation in its velocity-only form: no score network is trained. At inference we integrate the probability-flow ODE over 50 uniform Euler steps at guidance scale w = 3.0, and HiFi-GAN renders the result — the sampler is fully deterministic. The bridge scale σ = 0.2 is the only quantity that separates SB-CFM from optimal-transport flow matching; setting σ = 0 recovers OT-CFM exactly, which is why the ablations below can vary the bridge on its own.

§ V — Demo

Three clips per scene, one envelope per column

Every clip is four seconds, monophonic, 22.05 kHz. Within a column, all temporally conditioned systems were given the same RMS envelope, taken from the original recording in that column; PixelSNAIL and AudioLDM take no envelope. A single classifier-free guidance scale w = 3.0 is used throughout — every baseline at its published default, and all of our own variants sharing the same w. Systems are compared as published rather than under a shared vocoder: SB-CFM, OT-CFM, I-CFM, and PixelSNAIL use the official DCASE HiFi-GAN vocoder, T-Foley and MambaFoley synthesize waveforms directly, and AudioLDM uses the vocoder shipped with its own pipeline.

§ V — Results

Seven metrics, because no single one covers fidelity, diversity, and timing

§ VI — Ablation and analysis

Which part is actually doing the work

We decompose the result rather than defend it, along two axes — the coupling and the bridge scale σ — varying one at a time at fixed guidance and a fixed deterministic Euler sampler. The nesting SB-CFM ⊃ OT-CFM ⊃ I-CFM makes the first rows exact: the intra-class optimal-transport coupling supplies the larger remaining step, and the bridge improves on it up to an interior optimum at σ=0.2, beyond which the four distributional and diversity metrics turn together.

Table 3 Anatomy of the flow formulation

Each row changes one factor with respect to the row above, at fixed architecture, conditioning, guidance, and a deterministic 50-step Euler sampler; the σ column carries the bridge, and by construction the first two rows are exactly I-CFM and OT-CFM. The intra-class optimal-transport coupling supplies the larger of the two steps (I-CFM → OT-CFM: FAD 4.53 → 3.56, roughly 54% of the 1.78 that separates I-CFM from the full model). Two margins carry that claim — FAD falls by 0.97 and accuracy rises by 0.02; density rises by 0.155, and coverage, CLAP, and KAD move in the same direction by margins at or inside the run-to-run variation the σ sweep exposes. The bridge then improves on that up to an interior optimum at σ=0.2 (FAD 3.56 → 2.75, the remaining 46%), where FAD, KAD, density, and coverage all turn together; at σ=0.3 they reverse, while CLAP — which dips at σ=0.1 before rising — keeps climbing, so semantic alignment does not turn with the distributional metrics.

Limitations. On CLAP, semantic alignment to the class name, SB-CFM (0.338) does not overtake AudioLDM (0.358), and CLAP — which dips at σ=0.1 before rising — keeps climbing past the FAD optimum (0.340 at σ=0.3) — so semantic alignment does not turn with the distributional metrics, and the choice of operating point rests on the axes that agree rather than on all of them. AudioLDM is a general-purpose text-to-audio model whose large-scale language–audio pre-training is expected to favour this text-embedding metric, which our class-conditional model, trained only on this small corpus, does not directly optimize. The broadband moving motor vehicle category remains the principal headroom for every model — our own value there, 5.84, is 2.1 times the mean it contributes to, even though it is now below every baseline — and the per-scene breakdown locates the difficulty in sustained broadband content rather than in textures as a group. Results come from a single training run without seed averaging, so small margins should be read as indicative; evaluation relies on objective and automatic perceptual metrics rather than human listening tests.
